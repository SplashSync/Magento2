The Splash module connects your **Magento 2 | Adobe Commerce store** to your other applications
(ERP, CRM, other online stores) and keeps your data continuously in sync, with no double entry
and no manual export. :rocket:

### What gets synchronized

| Magento object | What is supported |
|---|---|
| Customers | Identity, email, company, addresses |
| Addresses | Billing and shipping addresses, phone |
| Products | Catalog, SKU, variants, prices, stocks, images, descriptions, SEO |
| Orders | Lines, statuses, addresses, carrier and tracking number |
| Invoices | Lines, totals excl. and incl. tax, statuses (read only) |

### Key features

- :office: **Multi-website**: choose the default website and attribute set for objects created
  from your other applications.
- :truck: **Order statuses**: map your Magento statuses to Splash standard statuses, with a
  dedicated logistic mode.
- :gear: **Simple setup**: a few parameters in the store configuration, or straight from the
  command line.

### Compatibility

- Magento **2.4** | Adobe Commerce
- PHP **7.4** to **8.1**
- An active Splash Sync account
